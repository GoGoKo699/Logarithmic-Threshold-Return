# Physical premises and evidence still required

**4 October 2026. Imported-result boundary, not a new literature search.** This table
organizes the limitations already stated in the supplied core, scope and prior-art
notes. It does not upgrade an abstract to full text or a component to a device.

| Premise or resource | Current basis | Remaining obligation |
|---|---|---|
| Coherent single-particle lattice dynamics | Explicit Hamiltonian; existing lattice addressing and quantum-walk component records in PRIOR_ART | Identify an appropriate single-band, low-loss realization without claiming the current model is an implemented device |
| One controlled attractive site and quadratic cycle | Exact mathematical protocol | Relate depth to a calibrated physical control; account for residual depth and competing confinement |
| Endpoint bound-state preparation | Defined initial vector and exact pole equation | Supply an actual preparation method and its error/cost |
| Large coherent area and duration | Finite-volume sufficient bound and finite examples | Establish coherent area/time, boundaries and other trapping potentials for a device claim |
| Positive-minimum window | Joint asymptotic away from b=1 | Do not interpret it as immunity to every fixed residual attraction |
| Return measurement | Bound-state projector, not origin-site occupation | Implement or calibrate that measurement; site-resolved imaging alone is not equivalent |
| Fixed positive anisotropy | Exact resolvent and checked matching hypotheses | Not a claim for a simultaneously vanishing hopping, arbitrary trap, extra band or disorder |
| Unitary evolution and no incoming continuum population | Conditional source model | Different initial populations or loss channels require their own analysis |
