# Closest predecessors and the remaining claim

**4 October 2026. Bounded construction-level comparison.** The following are the primary records actually retrieved in this pass, and the narrower passages inspected for this decision. This is not a new independent audit of every proof in them. Poorly matched or empty web searches are not counted as evidence that no precedent exists. No third-party PDF is redistributed.

## P1. Sokolovski and Pons: the direct foundation

*Adiabaticity in a time dependent trap: a passage near continuum threshold*, author preprint arXiv:1506.04019v2; published PRA 92, 042121 (2015).

[Primary full text](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1506.04019) · [Published identifier](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.92.042121)

Section II sets up a one-dimensional finite-range well and Eq. (6) a quadratic round trip. Section III derives the Sturmian system; Section IV Eqs. (21)–(26) identify auxiliary reflection with physical return and explain below-threshold gapped recovery. Section V gives the 38% touching result. These supply the problem's direct foundation. The log-edge scalar reduction is a specialization of the method, not a newly invented transform. The inspected one-dimensional finite-range/power-law threshold result does not state the present logarithmic lattice law. A paper framed merely as “slow trapping can lose a particle” would not be novel.

## P2. The variable-power universality result

Sokolovski and Pons, *Adiabaticity in a time-dependent trap: a universal limit for the loss by touching the continuum*, PRA 94, 013410 (2016), arXiv:1603.08718.

[Primary full text](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1603.08718) · [Publisher](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.94.013410)

The crucial hypothesis in Section VII, Eq. (24), is a bound energy approaching as a fixed finite power of time. Eqs. (25)–(27) use that threshold form to derive a duration-independent limiting probability. It is not enough to call that source “one dimensional”: its universality reasoning is the relevant potential subsumption. Our essential weak-binding approach is flatter than every fixed power. Substituting a duration-dependent effective exponent is not a justified uniform estimate or a derivation of the pi^2/4 coefficient. The older three-dimensional zero-range 38% result credited in the opening discussion is inherited background as well; the value is not universally exclusive to one dimension.

## P3. Eigenvalues that actually enter the continuum

Cornean, Jensen, Knorr and Nenciu, *On the adiabatic theorem when eigenvalues dive into the continuum*, arXiv:1612.02354.

[Primary full text](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1612.02354)

Theorem 1.3 and its following remarks use a normalizable critical state, with a norm-continuous limiting projection, and a class that enters the continuum over a nonzero parameter interval. The inspected theorem establishes ionization under those hypotheses. The present state spreads without a norm-continuous threshold projector and only touches the threshold in the zero-minimum cycle. This is a hypothesis mismatch, not a claim that the predecessor is irrelevant or that none of its techniques could extend. The prior scout07 projection-overlap test remains the explicit check on our side.

## P4. Two-dimensional complete ionization under continuing periodic forcing

Borrelli, Carlone and Tentarelli, *Complete ionization for a non-autonomous point interaction model in d=2*, arXiv:2108.06564.

[Primary full text](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2108.06564)

The monochromatic assumption Eq. (14) fixes a sinusoidal drive for all times. Theorem 1.3, Eqs. (16)–(17), describes a survival-amplitude asymptotic with logarithmic-over-time terms; the surrounding remarks state restrictions on the forcing and nonresonance. That is long observation time under a continuing periodic drive, not a slow single quadratic cycle. Their point-interaction extension parameter is not our on-site attraction. Neither their logarithms nor the shared label “2D ionization” imply the present return law. They prevent any broad claim that driven two-dimensional ionization was previously unknown.

## P5. Static lattice-resolvent methods

A. J. Guttmann, *Lattice Green functions in all dimensions*, arXiv:1004.1435.

[Primary full text](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1004.1435)

The Brillouin-zone and elliptic-integral treatment of lattice Green functions is established background. The present pass derives and independently checks the needed rectangular-lattice formula; it does not claim priority for that static evaluation. The candidate novelty is the controlled dynamic return law and its domain. The fixed-anisotropy scope corollary follows by checking the existing matching hypotheses, not by declaring every system with a logarithm equivalent.

## What the current search did not establish

Forward and backward searches did not produce a directly matching general logarithmic-return theorem. Several restrictive queries instead produced irrelevant material. None of that is evidence of absence. The older A. Z. Devdariani reference, Theoretical and Mathematical Physics 11, 460 (1972), is known here through P2's citation and discussion; this pass did not obtain a readable primary full text. It has not been independently excluded at construction level. No title or detailed claim is invented for it.

The result that survives the inspected comparisons is a **controlled inverse-square-logarithmic return law for a quadratic near-threshold local trap, including explicit residual-depth and finite-volume limits**. That is not the first occurrence of adiabatic failure, continuum escape, a Sturmian method, 2D weak binding, or a local-propagation estimate. The fixed-positive-hopping extension tests whether equal hoppings are an accidental requirement; it is supporting scope rather than a second headline.

These are component-level precedents, not a completed five-to-ten-primary-paper audit of each load-bearing premise. This pass does not advertise a jointly demonstrated apparatus or fill the gap by citing unrelated experiments. The preparation, local-depth calibration, competing confinement, coherent area/time, single-band validity and bound-state projection still require their explicit evidence. A mathematically specified family can be developed before a prototype is claimed.
